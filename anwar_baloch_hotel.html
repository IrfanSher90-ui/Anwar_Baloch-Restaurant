OUR HERITAGE

A Legacy of Desi Flavour

Anwar Baloch Restaurant is known for its authentic desi flavors including chicken karahi, beef karahi, prawn karahi, and traditional dishes served fresh.

Every dish crafted on open flame — the way Karachi has eaten for generations. We've been serving families with unmatched taste and affordable prices since 1998.
